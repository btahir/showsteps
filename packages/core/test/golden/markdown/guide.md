# Update billing settings in Acme

Sign in, switch to **monthly** billing, and pay the open invoice.

## 1. Go to **Sign in** on Acme

![Step 1: Go to Sign in on Acme](images/s_open.png)

*Page: [Sign in – Acme](https://app.acme.test/login)*

## 2. Type "jane@example.com" in **Email**

![Step 2: Type "jane@example.com" in Email](images/s_email.png)

## 3. Enter your password

![Step 3: Enter your password](images/s_pass.png)

## 4. Click **Sign in**

![Step 4: Click Sign in](images/s_signin.png)

## 5. Click **Open settings**

The gear icon in the top-right corner.

![Step 5: Click Open settings](images/s_gear.png)

*Page: [Dashboard – Acme](https://app.acme.test/dashboard)*

## 6. Select **Monthly** in **Billing period**

Choose how often you want to be billed.

![Step 6: Select Monthly in Billing period](images/s_period.png)

*Page: [Billing – Acme](https://app.acme.test/settings/billing)*

## 7. Check **Email me invoices**

![Step 7: Check Email me invoices](images/s_invoices.png)

## 8. Click **Pay now**

![Step 8: Click Pay now](images/s_pay.png)

## 9. Go to **Invoices** on Acme Help

![Step 9: Go to Invoices on Acme Help](images/s_help.png)

*Page: [Invoices – Acme Help](https://help.acme.test/invoices)*

## 10. Press **Enter** to search

![Step 10: Press Enter to search](images/s_search.png)
