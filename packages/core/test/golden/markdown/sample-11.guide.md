# Sign in, ask for help, and update billing in Acme Books

Sign in, look something up in the help centre in a second tab, then switch the **billing period** to yearly and save.

## 1. Go to **Sign in** on Acme Books

![Step 1: Go to Sign in on Acme Books](images/s00.png)

*Page: [Sign in – Acme Books](http://127.0.0.1:4517/index.html)*

## 2. Type "jane@example.com" in **Email**

![Step 2: Type "jane@example.com" in Email](images/s01.png)

## 3. Enter your password

![Step 3: Enter your password](images/s02.png)

## 4. Click **Sign in**

![Step 4: Click Sign in](images/s03.png)

## 5. Click **Help centre**

![Step 5: Click Help centre](images/s04.png)

*Page: [Dashboard – Acme Books](http://127.0.0.1:4517/dashboard.html)*

## 6. Search for "invoices"

![Step 6: Search for "invoices"](images/s05.png)

*Page: [Help centre – Acme Books](http://127.0.0.1:4517/help.html)*

## 7. Click **Settings**

![Step 7: Click Settings](images/s07.png)

*Page: [Reports – Acme Books](http://127.0.0.1:4517/dashboard/reports)*

## 8. Select **Yearly** in **Billing period**

![Step 8: Select Yearly in Billing period](images/s08.png)

*Page: [Settings – Acme Books](http://127.0.0.1:4517/settings.html)*

## 9. Check **Email me invoices**

![Step 9: Check Email me invoices](images/s09.png)

## 10. Click **Save**

![Step 10: Click Save](images/s10.png)
