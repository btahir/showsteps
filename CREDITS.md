# Credits

## Fonts

- **Source Sans 3** (Regular 400 and Bold 700; Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, Cyrillic Extended and Greek subsets via `@fontsource/source-sans-3` 5.3.0): (c) Adobe / Google, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Default font for PDF export. Files and licence text: `packages/core/assets/fonts/` (`OFL.txt`). The fonts are unmodified.
- **Rethink Sans** (variable, weights 400 to 800; Latin and Latin Extended subsets) and **Fragment Mono** (Regular 400, Latin subset): SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-RethinkSans.txt`, `OFL-FragmentMono.txt`). Embedded as base64 in the exported HTML guide; the PDF uses static 400/700 instances of Rethink Sans (Latin, Latin Extended) and Fragment Mono Regular from `@fontsource/rethink-sans` and `@fontsource/fragment-mono` 5.3.0. Copies of the brand font files: `packages/core/assets/fonts/` (originals in `packages/brand/fonts/`). The fonts are unmodified.

## Launch media (apps/launch)

- **Soundtrack (launch video).** Original work, generated by `apps/launch/audio/make-track.mjs`: a deterministic 30 s bed (pad, plucks, bass, soft kick and hats) plus small UI sounds, synthesised in plain Node from sine, triangle and noise oscillators, then encoded to MP3 with ffmpeg. It uses no samples, loops or third-party audio, so it is released under this project's MIT licence. The MP3 itself is gitignored (`docs/launch/media/track.mp3`); anyone can rebuild it from the script. The owner may replace it with any 30 s track: put the file at `docs/launch/media/track.mp3` and render without `--new-audio`.
- **Footage and screenshots.** Recorded from the Showsteps extension driving the mock "Acme Books" app in `apps/fixtures` (fictional company, `jane@example.com`, a made-up password). Nothing is taken from a real product, brand or person.
- **Motion design.** Original HTML/CSS/GSAP compositions in `apps/launch/video` and `apps/launch/hero`. Fonts are the brand fonts above.
- **Tools, not shipped.** [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) (Apache-2.0) renders the compositions to video; it is a dev dependency of the private `@stepsnap/launch` package only. [GSAP](https://gsap.com) is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN while rendering and is not bundled or redistributed (GreenSock standard licence). ffmpeg and libwebp (`cwebp`, `img2webp`) encode the results.
- **README and store screenshots.** `docs/screenshots/*.webp` (committed) and `docs/launch/store/*.png` (gitignored) are captures of the extension and of exports made from the mock recording, re-encoded to WebP or PNG, plus the layout cards built by `apps/launch/docs-shots` and `apps/launch/store`. They contain no real people, products or data.
- **Images generated with an image model:** none. Every raster asset comes from the product itself.
